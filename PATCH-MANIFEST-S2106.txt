S2106 — Modal/Form UI Hierarchy + Mobile Hardening

Scope:
- Global modal component presentation: spacing, title hierarchy, field sizing, action-button layout, and narrow-screen density.
- Prevent modal/form children from forcing horizontal overflow.
- Keep close controls, inputs, selects, textareas, and action buttons usable on touch devices.
- <=600px: tighter modal padding and 2-column action rows.
- <=360px: further compact spacing and 2-column 3-way toggle layout.

Safety:
- CSS-only; no runtime JS, persistence, SOT, data model, business logic, or dependency changes.
- No theme definitions or theme-specific color changes.
- Reuses existing .modal/.fg/.fi/.fs/.btn-row primitives.

Regression:
- Added tests/s2106-modal-form-ui.test.js.
